Brand Mark & Visual Symbol

Logo & Emblem Architecture

The brandmark combines the iconic Academic Graduation Cap (🎓) with an interconnected network pill badge, symbolizing the continuous web of alumni distributed across the global workforce.

🎓 ALUMNI Alumni Tracking System
Primary Mark: High-contrast gradient badge paired with Plus Jakarta Sans bold logotype wordmark.

Emblem Rules & Specifications

  • ✅ Corner Radius: 9999px pill geometry for the gradient badge.
  • ✅ Clearance Zone: Minimum margin of 16px on all sides when placed alongside third-party marks.
  • ✅ Dark Canvas Display: Optimized for deep velvet plum (#1a101e) and pure black surfaces.
  • ❌ No Distortions: Never alter the core brand colors or invert gradient directions.

Voice & Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans & 17px Editorial Pace

Tight display tracking for headlines paired with relaxed, legible body reading pace.

Display Headline Standard
The Signature "Apple-Tight" Voice

Used for hero banners, tile headers, and modal titles. Characterized by negative letter-spacing for confident authority.

17px Body Reading Pace
Default paragraphs are tuned to exactly 17px with 1.47–1.5 line height for maximum scanning comfort across mobile and desktop.

Technical tags and ID numbers rely on JetBrains Mono for unambiguous character distinction.
